Government Schemes

Information about various government schemes available for citizens through the Gram Panchayat.

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana

This scheme provides permanent houses to economically weaker and needy families.

Swachh Bharat Mission

A scheme implemented to promote cleanliness, toilet facilities, and create a clean village.

Jal Jeevan Mission

To provide safe and adequate drinking water through tap connections to every rural household.

MGNREGA

This scheme provides employment opportunities to citizens in rural areas.
