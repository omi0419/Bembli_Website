History of Bembli Village

Bembli is a well-known village located in Dharashiv Taluka, Dharashiv District, Maharashtra, India. The village has long been recognized for its agricultural heritage, peaceful surroundings, and close-knit community. Over the years, Bembli has developed steadily while preserving its traditional values and cultural identity.

Our Village Journey

Agriculture has always been the backbone of the village economy. The fertile land and the hard work of local farmers have played a significant role in the growth and prosperity of the village.

Along with farming, the people of Bembli actively participate in social, cultural and religious activities that strengthen community bonds.

According to the Census records, Bembli has a population of 11,339 people living in 2,188 households, covering an area of approximately 20.88 square kilometres (2, 087.69 hectares). The village is located about 25 kilometres from Tuljapur, making it well connected to nearby towns and essential services.

Today, Bembli continues to progress with improved educational facilities, infrastructure public services, and community development initiatives while maintaining its rich traditions and rural heritage. The village stands as a symbol of unity, hard work, and continuous development.
