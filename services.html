Citizen Guidelines

  • Carry original documents along with photocopies.
  • Submit accurate and complete information.
  • Keep your acknowledgement receipt safely.
  • Visit the office during working hours only.
  • Contact the Gram Panchayat for any assistance.
  • Additional documents may be required depending on the service.
